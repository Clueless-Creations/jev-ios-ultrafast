# Capture protocol

## Capture the atlas first

Do one discovery pass before detailed measurement. Identify distinct state and interaction families, then choose representative probes.

## Evidence bundle

For every important interaction:

1. restore a known prerequisite state;
2. capture a before screenshot;
3. capture semantics/accessibility information when available;
4. start recording;
5. hold briefly before input;
6. perform one interaction;
7. let it settle;
8. hold briefly after completion;
9. stop recording;
10. capture final screenshot;
11. record the resulting state and any feedback;
12. repeat timing-sensitive interactions.

Use short clips. Full walkthroughs are indexes, not primary measurement evidence.

## Motion probes

Capture:

- normal speed;
- slow drag when interactive;
- fast drag;
- partial release;
- cancellation;
- reversal;
- repeated trials;
- Reduce Motion when relevant.

## Gesture probes

Test touch ownership and conflict boundaries. Record whether nested scroll, sheet, pager, edge gesture, or component wins.

## Privacy

Keep raw captures private by default. Redact before sending to remote models. Redaction is a derived artifact and does not replace the original evidence.
